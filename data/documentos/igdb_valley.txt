RESUMEN:
Explore the vast and beautiful world of Valley using the power of the L.E.A.F. suit: a fierce exoskeleton that grants exceptional speed and agility along with the phenomenal ability to manipulate the life and death of all living things.

HISTORIA:
Valley is a first-person action-adventure that sends the player deep into a secluded, forgotten valley hidden within the Rocky Mountains. There, amid forests, ruins, and lingering traces of the past, lies an abandoned military base dating back to the Second World War, the site of secretive experiments long since left to decay. Exploring the area, the player uncovers a remarkable relic of those experiments: the L.E.A.F. suit, a piece of wartime technology that grants its wearer extraordinary, superhuman abilities of speed and agility. Empowered by the suit, the player bounds and leaps across the sprawling wilderness, delving ever deeper into the valley's mysteries. But the discovery comes with a strange and weighty burden, for the wearer becomes intimately bound to the valley itself, gaining the power to take life and to give it back, a gift that cannot be wielded without consequence. What begins as exploration soon becomes a journey into the secrets the valley has kept hidden.
