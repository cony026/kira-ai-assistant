RESUMEN:
Wandersong takes you on a whimsical, musical journey across the globe. Eons ago, the goddess Eya put the universe into motion with her music--now, as she does every epoch or so, she's going to sing a new song and reset existence. You play as a lowly bard tasked with finding the pieces of a mysterious melody called the Earthsong, which is said to be able to preserve the planet. Use your singing to help characters, solve puzzles, and save the world!

HISTORIA:
Wandersong is a whimsical, music-driven puzzle adventure that follows a cheerful wandering singer known simply as the Bard. In a dream, the Bard is visited by a messenger of the goddess Eya, who delivers grave news: the world is coming to an end. The Bard is gently told that they are not the prophesied Hero destined to save it, but they may still have a part to play. There exists a legendary composition called the Earthsong, a melody that, when sung in perfect harmony by everyone in the world, has the power to preserve the universe itself. Pieces of this song are kept by seven mystical Overseers scattered across the land, each residing in their own corner of the spirit world. Armed only with their voice, the Bard sets off on a globe-spanning journey, joined by a sharp-tongued witch named Miriam, singing to solve puzzles and befriending the many people they meet as they seek out each fragment of the Earthsong.
