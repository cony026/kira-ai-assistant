RESUMEN:
NTE: Neverness to Everness is a supernatural open-world RPG developed by Hotta Studio. The story begins in the city of Hethereau, where you play as its first "unlicensed" Anomaly Hunter. You'll join the crew at Eibon, a rundown antique shop that keeps the lights on by taking Anomaly commissions from anyone willing to pay. Alongside a diverse cast of extraordinary companions, you'll unravel urban mysteries, ride out adventures full of laughs and heartbreak, and write your own stories in the neon-lit streets.

HISTORIA:
In the modern metropolis of Hethereau, supernatural anomalies have become part of everyday life, ranging from minor disturbances to dangerous phenomena. An Appraiser working out of Eibon, a struggling antique shop that takes anomaly commissions to stay afloat, investigates these occurrences alongside a diverse group of companions who each possess their own Esper powers. As the investigations deepen, the team uncovers a layered web of connections behind the anomalies, revealing how they affect different parts of the city and its inhabitants.
