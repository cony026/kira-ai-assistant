DESCRIPCIÓN / RESEÑA:
Set on the planet Carcosa under the threat of an ominous eclipse, take on the role of Arjun Devraj, a powerful Soltari Enforcer searching for answers on a lost off-world colony.

SAROS features permanent progression systems, where every death reshapes the world while offering new upgrades to overcome challenges.

With a haunting story, deep third-person action, and a grounded performance by Rahul Kohli, SAROS presents the next evolution in Housemarque's gameplay-first experience.
