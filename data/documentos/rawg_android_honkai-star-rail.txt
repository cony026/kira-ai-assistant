DESCRIPCIÓN:
Honkai: Star Rail is a free-to-play, turn-based RPG from the creators of Genshin Impact. Players travel across the galaxy aboard the Astral Express, recruiting a roster of characters organized into different combat Paths and battling through space-fantasy story chapters. New characters and story content are added on a regular update schedule through a gacha system.
