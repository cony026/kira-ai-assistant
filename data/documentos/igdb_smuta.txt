RESUMEN:
Smuta is a third-person action/RPG computer game developed by the Russian game studio Cyberia Nova.

HISTORIA:
The game takes place in 1612, during the Time of Troubles. The plot is based on the novel by Mikhail Zagoskin “Yuri Miloslavsky, or the Russians in 1612” . However, as the developers note, “the game script will be more dynamic than the literary source”.
