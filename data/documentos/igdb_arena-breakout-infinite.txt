RESUMEN:
Arena Breakout: Infinite is a tactical extraction shooter featuring PvPvE combat. Players enter raids where they fight both AI enemies and other players, collect loot, and attempt to escape the map alive. Failing to extract means losing all acquired gear. The game emphasizes realistic visuals and sound design.
