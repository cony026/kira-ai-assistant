RESUMEN:
Absolver is an online multiplayer combat RPG where players are placed behind the mask of a Prospect under control of the Guides, the new rulers of the fallen Adal Empire, who have placed you here to determine your worth in joining their elite corps of Absolvers.

HISTORIA:
Absolver is a martial arts action role-playing game set in the fictional, fallen land of Adal. Players take on the role of a masked warrior called a Prospect, one of many wanderers seeking to prove their worth and earn a place among the peacekeeping order known as the Absolvers. Bound to a mask that suppresses hunger and fatigue, the Prospect roams the ruins of a collapsed empire, testing their skills against other fighters and honing their own personal combat style. The journey is as much about self-discovery and finding one's place in a broken world as it is about mastering the art of the fight.
