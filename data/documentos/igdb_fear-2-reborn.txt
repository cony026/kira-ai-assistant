RESUMEN:
Experience four brand new single-player levels in this additional DLC campaign for F.E.A.R.2! Coinciding with the events seen in the full game, you will experience the chaos of Alma’s aftermath from an entirely new point of view: that of Replica Soldier 813.

HISTORIA:
The player takes the role of Foxtrot 813, a Replica soldier guided by Paxton Fettel, the antagonist of the original game. Paxton turn on his fellow Replica and try to reach the epicentre of the nuclear explosion triggered by the F.E.A.R. Point Man's failed attempt to contain Alma. However she is striving to stop Foxtrot 813 by sending ghostly creations against him.
