RESUMEN:
An aesthetic adventure with the premise of enjoying the little things!
Toem is an adventure game, it aims to capture the curiosity of a child! Should I go left, right, who cares I'm on an adventure! Your smile, that's what's important to us.

HISTORIA:
As players wander around TOEM, they hear stories from other adventurers. They talk about serenity, the ever-gazing eyes, and the pillars up high. An aesthetic journey inside a mysterious world. Stories, secrets, and undiscovered creatures. That’s what TOEM is all about.
