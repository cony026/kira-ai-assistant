RESUMEN:
Like a Dragon: Infinite Wealth is a turn-based role-playing game developed by Ryu Ga Gotoku Studio and published by Sega. It follows dual protagonists Ichiban Kasuga and Kazuma Kiryu, with the narrative set primarily in Japan and Hawaii. The game uses a party-based combat system that builds on previous Like a Dragon entries, incorporating positioning and environmental interactions. Players engage in main story missions alongside a wide range of optional activities and side content. The title combines narrative-driven progression with exploration, character development, and minigames typical of the series.

HISTORIA:
Years after leaving the criminal underworld behind, former yakuza Ichiban Kasuga works at an employment agency in Yokohama, dedicating himself to helping other ex-gangsters find honest, legitimate work and carrying on the legacy of his late father figure. His hard-won stability collapses when a VTuber spreads false rumors that he is building his own crime ring, costing Kasuga and his friends their jobs. As he tries to clear his name amid a sweeping effort to disband Japan's remaining yakuza clans, Kasuga is stunned by a revelation: his biological mother, whom he never knew, is alive and living in Honolulu, Hawaii. Determined to find her, he sets off across the Pacific, a deeply personal journey that will pull him into a sprawling new drama far from home.
