DESCRIPCIÓN / RESEÑA:
Dakar Desert Rally is the biggest and most epic off-road rally racing adventure ever developed.

Dakar Desert Rally captures the genuine speed and excitement of Amaury Sport Organisation's largest rally race on the planet, featuring a wide variety of licensed vehicles from the world's top makers, including motorcycles, cars, trucks, quads and SSVs. The game offers unique challenges for off-road simulation diehards and casual racing fans alike.

Compete in fierce online multiplayer races or navigate the vast wilds in single-player offline.
