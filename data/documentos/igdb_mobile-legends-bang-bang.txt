RESUMEN:
Mobile Legends: Bang Bang is a multiplayer online battle arena (MOBA) game designed for smartphones and tablets. Two teams of five players compete in real time across a three-lane map, aiming to destroy the opposing base while defending their own. Players choose from over 130 heroes divided into six roles (Tank, Marksman, Assassin, Fighter, Mage, and Support) and climb a ranked system from Warrior to Mythic.
