HISTORIA:
In the bustling hub of Conton City, watched over by the mysterious Supreme Kai of Time and her bird Tokitoki, an elite group known as the Time Patrol guards the flow of history itself. The player becomes one of these patrollers, a custom warrior of one of the Dragon Ball universe's storied races, tasked by the Elder Kai with mending dangerous distortions rippling through time. Someone is meddling with the past, empowering old villains and twisting the outcomes of legendary battles. Behind these changes lurk the scheming sorceress Towa and her monstrous creation Mira, who draw dark power from altered timelines to further their own sinister ends. Journeying alongside heroes like Goku, Vegeta, and Future Trunks across iconic eras of the saga, the patroller must set right each corrupted moment before history unravels. As the meddling grows bolder, the fate of every timeline rests on the shoulders of a single rising warrior.
