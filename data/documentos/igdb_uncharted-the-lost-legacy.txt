RESUMEN:
Uncharted: The Lost Legacy is a single-player action-adventure game played from a third-person perspective, and a standalone expansion in the Uncharted series. Players guide treasure hunter Chloe Frazer through towns, ruins, and temples across the Western Ghats of India, using firearms, melee combat, and stealth against hostile forces. Gameplay combines gunfights, climbing and platforming, environmental puzzles, and vehicle sections across larger, more open environments than earlier entries. It also grants access to the multiplayer component of Uncharted 4.

HISTORIA:
During an insurgency in India, treasure hunter Chloe Frazer sets out to recover the fabled Tusk of Ganesh, an artifact her late father died searching for. To reach it she partners with the hardened mercenary Nadine Ross, and the two slip past the forces of Asav, a ruthless warlord who wants the relic to rally the country into civil war. Following a trail of clues through the mountains of the Western Ghats toward the ancient Hoysala Empire, the pair must rely on each other's very different skills while staying ahead of Asav and his men.
