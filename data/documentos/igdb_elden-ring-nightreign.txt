RESUMEN:
Elden Ring: Nightreign is a standalone adventure within the ELDEN RING universe, crafted to offer players a new gaming experience by reimagining the game’s core design.

HISTORIA:
In a mysterious, ever-shifting land known as Limveld, a reshaped and unfamiliar version of the once-familiar realm of Limgrave, an encroaching darkness called the Night threatens to consume all. Into this peril step the Nightfarers, wandering warriors drawn together at a sanctuary called the Roundtable Hold. The tale begins with the Wylder, a greatsword-wielding Nightfarer fleeing through the dark before confronting a dreaded figure known as the Fell Omen. Waking at the Roundtable Hold, the Wylder is greeted by the enigmatic Priestess, who reveals the Nightfarers' grim purpose: to hunt down and destroy the powerful Nightlords and put an end to the Night itself. Joined by a band of distinct and battle-hardened companions, the Wylder sets out to brave a hostile world and challenge the terrors that rule it.
