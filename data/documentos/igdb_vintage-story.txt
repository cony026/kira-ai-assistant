RESUMEN:
Vintage Story is an uncompromising wilderness survival sandbox game that finds a fine line between hardcore survival and a cozy sandbox. Players will awaken to a ruined medieval world reclaimed by nature and permeated by eldritch, temporal disturbances as they gather resources to prepare for a harsh, approaching winter.
