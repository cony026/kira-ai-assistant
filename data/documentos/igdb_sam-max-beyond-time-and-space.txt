RESUMEN:
Join the Freelance Police for a second season of adventure, crime fighting, and chaos. Sam & Max Beyond Time and Space starts with a giant robot attack and never lets up, taking our valiant crime fighters on a gut-wrenching mission from the North Pole to Easter Island, from outer space to Hell and back. Babies will dance. Mariachis will sing. A giant battle robot will trash the streets. And when their friends are caught in the middle, Sam & Max risk their very souls to set things right.

HISTORIA:
A year after their previous adventure, the Freelance Police duo Sam, a level-headed dog detective, and Max, his manic rabbit-like partner, find their quiet routine shattered when their pet goldfish Mr. Spatula inexplicably turns evil, only to be destroyed by a giant robot dispatched by Santa Claus. Determined to get answers, the pair travel to the North Pole, where they find Santa acting violently and suspect something sinister has taken hold of him. So begins a sprawling series of bizarre cases that will hurl the two detectives beyond the bounds of time and space, from the frozen North to a remote island and beyond. Confronting demons, prophecies, and stranger threats still, Sam and Max follow one outlandish mystery after another wherever it leads.
