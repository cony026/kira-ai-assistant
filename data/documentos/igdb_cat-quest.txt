RESUMEN:
Cat Quest is a 2D open world RPG with the exploration of Skyrim, but set in the visual style of an overworld. This is a fantasy world that asks the question of how different it would be if cats lived in it instead of humans!
Go to places like the Catpital where the Lion King rules, or experience delicacies like the catnip pie! Talk to the locals, some already on their 7th or 8th lives(what? not all cats have nine lives y'know), and go on a catventure through a world you've never seen before!

HISTORIA:
Cat Quest is a lighthearted action role-playing game set in Felingard, a charming open-world kingdom populated entirely by cats. The story begins when the hero's sister is snatched away at sea by a mysterious white cat, leaving him shipwrecked and washed ashore on Felingard's coast. Guided by a spirit companion named Spirry, he discovers that he is a Dragonblood, heir to a legendary line of warriors who once vanquished the realm's dragons. To win back his sister, the hero sets out across Felingard to hunt down its dragons, exploring dungeons and ancient ruins that slowly reveal the kingdom's forgotten history.
