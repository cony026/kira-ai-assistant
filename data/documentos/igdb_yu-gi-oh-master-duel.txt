RESUMEN:
Yu-Gi-Oh! Master Duel lets players old and new enjoy the hugely popular Yu-Gi-Oh! TCG in digital form. Players across the globe can compete in intense collectible card Duels, both thrilling to play and watch. Alongside those iconic cards will be a huge range of other monsters, spells, traps, and more, drawn from Yu-Gi-Oh's two-decade-long legacy.

HISTORIA:
Yu-Gi-Oh! Master Duel will have something for everyone. Fans of the trading card game will be both wowed by how the game plays and how exciting it is to watch, balancing presentation with the tempo of the Duels. If you’re a new player or even a Duelist who may need a refresher course, there’s an in-game tutorial to help you on your path to Yu-Gi-Oh! glory.
