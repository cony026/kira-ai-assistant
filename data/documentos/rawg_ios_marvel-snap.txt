DESCRIPCIÓN:
Marvel Snap is a fast-paced digital collectible card game where two players build small decks of Marvel characters and compete to control two of three locations after six turns. Matches typically last only a few minutes, and a risk/reward 'Snap' mechanic lets players double the stakes mid-match. New locations and cards are added on a monthly season schedule.
