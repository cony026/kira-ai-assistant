RESUMEN:
Remnant II is the sequel to the best-selling game Remnant: From the Ashes that pits survivors of humanity against new deadly creatures and god-like bosses across terrifying worlds. Play solo or co-op with two other friends to explore the depths of the unknown to stop an evil from destroying reality itself. To succeed, players will need to rely on their own skills and those of their team to overcome the toughest challenges and to stave off humanity’s extinction.

HISTORIA:
In a world ravaged by the Root, a destructive, plant-like species that spreads like a virus, a lone survivor known as the Traveler is rescued and brought to the last-refuge settlement of Ward 13. There they discover the World Stone, a crystal that opens portals to other realms, and are drawn into a desperate mission after companions vanish through it. Journeying across strange and hostile worlds, from Root-choked forests to a planet-sized starship, the Traveler searches for the missing, allies with a guardian known as the Keeper, and races to stop the Root before it consumes everything.
