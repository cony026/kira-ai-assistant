RESUMEN:
Titan Souls is an indie action-adventure game centered around battling powerful bosses in a minimalist world. Players are equipped with a single arrow and one hit point, emphasizing precision, timing, and strategic movement. The game features pixel-art visuals, atmospheric environments, and a focus on trial-and-error gameplay.

HISTORIA:
Titan Souls tells the story of a nameless warrior’s quest to bring down the ancient titans with nothing but a single, indestructible arrow. There are no lesser classes of enemies, no crafting, no grinding and no side quests -- just you and the two dozen titans scattered throughout the land awaiting your challenge.
