RESUMEN:
The Incredible Hulk is a free-roaming, open-world game, allowing the player to wreak havoc on the streets of New York City while playing through the story of the 2008 The Incredible Hulk film.

HISTORIA:
Based on the 2008 film, The Incredible Hulk drops players into an open-world recreation of Manhattan as scientist Bruce Banner, a man cursed by an experiment gone wrong. Whenever his anger boils over, Banner transforms into the Hulk, a massive and near-unstoppable green behemoth capable of leveling buildings and hurling vehicles with ease. Hunted and desperate, Banner is pursued relentlessly by the United States military, which sees the creature as a threat to be captured or destroyed. At the same time, he must contend with the shadowy Enclave organization, which harbors its own dangerous designs. Through it all, Banner is driven by a single overriding goal: to find a cure for the condition that turns him into the monster he fears becoming. As the Hulk, players tear through the city, smashing enemies and the environment alike while the military escalates its response with ever more powerful weapons, tanks, and specialized machines determined to bring the rampaging giant down.
