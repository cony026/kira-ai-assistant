HISTORIA:
Based on the film, the story catches up with the toys who once belonged to a boy named Andy: Woody, Buzz Lightyear, Jessie, and the rest of the gang. Now seventeen and leaving for college, Andy gives his beloved toys away to a young girl named Bonnie. Three of them, Hamm, Rex, and Slinky, recount to Bonnie's toys an adventure they had before arriving in her care. That tale takes the toys to Sunnyside Daycare, a colorful play center ruled by a stuffed bear named Lotso, where things turn out to be far less welcoming than they first appear. What follows is a journey of loyalty and friendship as the toys try to find their way back home.
