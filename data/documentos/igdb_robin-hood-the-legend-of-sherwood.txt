RESUMEN:
Prince John Lackland has stolen the throne from King Richard Lionheart and put him to jail. You play as Robin Hood and his band of Merry Men, fighting Prince John and the Sheriff of Nottingham.

Use the different skills of your Merry Men to either fight or sneak your way through levels to complete your objectives.
