RESUMEN:
Free Fire is the ultimate survival shooter game available on mobile. Each 10-minute game places you on a remote island where you are pit against 49 other players, all seeking survival. Players freely choose their starting point with their parachute, and aim to stay in the safe zone for as long as possible. Drive vehicles to explore the vast map, hide in the wild, or become invisible by proning under grass or rifts. Ambush, snipe, survive, there is only one goal: to survive and answer the call of duty.

HISTORIA:
Garena Free Fire is set in a dystopian future following a global event known as The Great Collapse, where the world is controlled by the authoritarian Horizon Corporation. This technology and genetics conglomerate abducts elite fighters, scientists, and rebels from across the globe, forcing them into mass survival experiments on isolated islands such as Bermuda. Horizon's ultimate goal is to engineer the perfect soldier through genetic profiling and cybernetic enhancements. The overarching narrative details the conflict between Horizon's tyrannical regime and The Mambas, a resistance group operating in the shadows of futuristic cities to sabotage the corporation and free the captives. The central plot follows characters like Hayato Yagami, a samurai heir torn between family tradition and rebellion, and Kelly, who undergoes the Awakening—a phenomenon where island prisoners break through their mental conditioning, regain their memories, and unlock their peak physical capabilities to fight for their freedom.
