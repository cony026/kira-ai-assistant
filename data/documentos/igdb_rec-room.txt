Rec Room is a virtual reality online game platform to build and play games together. Party up with friends from all around the world to chat, hang out, explore millions of player-created rooms, or build something new and amazing to share with us all.

On June 1, 2026, following financial issues within the Rec Room Inc. company, Rec Room officially ceased operations and shut down all game servers.
