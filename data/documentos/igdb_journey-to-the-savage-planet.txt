RESUMEN:
Onward To Adventure
In this colorful, co-op adventure game you play as the newest recruit to Kindred Aerospace. Dropped onto an uncharted planet with little equipment, you must determine if this planet is fit for human habitation but perhaps you are not the first to set foot here…

HISTORIA:
As the newest recruit of Kindred Aerospace, cheerfully billed as the "4th Best Interstellar Exploration Company," the player is dropped onto the uncharted alien world of ARY-26 with little equipment and a simple assignment: find out whether the planet is fit for human colonization. Landing amid vibrant, bizarre landscapes teeming with strange creatures both harmless and hostile, the explorer sets about cataloguing the local flora and fauna and scavenging resources. Crafting new gear like jetpacks and grappling hooks to reach ever more distant regions, the lone adventurer pushes deeper into the planet's mysteries in a tongue-in-cheek journey of cosmic discovery.
