DESCRIPCIÓN / RESEÑA:
If war is the closest we get to hell on earth, it's because Earth harbours the worst of demons: humankind.

In an isolated country ravaged by infighting, discover the secrets of your past and deal with the repercussions of a mysterious calamity.

Hell is Us is a 3rd-person action-adventure game that combines intense melee combat with the thrill of exploration. Explore a semi-open world to find answers to your questions and face mysterious creatures all along your journey.
