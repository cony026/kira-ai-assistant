RESUMEN:
This prequel to Vol. 1 provides a look at a day in the life of Shigure and the six catgirls in the family home. With little to no involvement of the previous protagonist Kashou Minaduki.

HISTORIA:
What's NEKOPARA? Why, it's a cat paradise!

This is a story just before Kashou opened "La Soleil".

The eldest daughter of the Minazuki household and their 6 catgirls go to wake up their master; prepare breakfast; clean up the house; go out on a walk; make dinner; take a bath; sleep together; et cetera, et cetera.

Enjoy a page from the leisurely, daily lives of Shigure and the Minazuki household's catgirls!
