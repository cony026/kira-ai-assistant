RESUMEN:
Fall Guys flings hordes of contestants together online in a mad dash through round after round of escalating chaos until one victor remains! Battle bizarre obstacles, shove through unruly competitors, and overcome the unbending laws of physics to avoid both humiliation and elimination. Leave your dignity at the door and prepare for hilarious failure in your quest for the crown.

HISTORIA:
Fall Guys takes place in a world where bean-shaped contestants are created for an endless televised game show known as the Blunderdome. These “Fall Guys” are spawned without memory and compete in chaotic obstacle courses and survival rounds, all for the purpose of winning Crowns, the ultimate prize.

As they are eliminated, contestants are immediately respawned or replaced by identical copies, keeping the competition eternal. The show is broadcast across the universe, and the Fall Guys exist solely to participate in these endless games designed as entertainment, with no escape or purpose outside of the competition itself.
