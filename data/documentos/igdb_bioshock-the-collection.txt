RESUMEN:
BioShock: The Collection is a game bundle consisting of remastered 1080p editions of BioShock, BioShock 2 and BioShock Infinite along with their accompanying downloadable content.

HISTORIA:
This collection gathers the complete BioShock trilogy, three interlinked tales of grand utopian dreams gone catastrophically wrong. The original BioShock and its sequel descend into Rapture, a secret art-deco metropolis built beneath the Atlantic Ocean by an idealist who sought a haven free from government and moral restraint. When a plane crash strands a lone survivor above its ruins, he discovers a once-glittering city collapsed into civil war, its citizens twisted into addicts by gene-altering serums called Plasmids, and its cavernous halls stalked by armored Big Daddies and the eerie little girls they protect. The trilogy's final chapter, BioShock Infinite, rises instead to Columbia, a majestic city floating among the clouds, where a former agent named Booker DeWitt is sent to retrieve a mysterious young woman named Elizabeth held captive there. Together these stories explore soaring ambition, ruin, and the dark ideologies that shape impossible cities.
