RESUMEN:
Welcome to Snake Pass; a physical action-puzzle game that sees the player slither, curl and climb their way through increasingly challenging worlds filled with evermore intricate obstacles and fiendishly mind-bending objectives!

Snake Pass is the wriggling brain-child of Seb Liese, who originally came to Sumo from Holland to work on the LittleBigPlanet team. His prototype of ‘real snake physics’ captured the entire studio’s imagination and we’re really proud of the results so far!

HISTORIA:
In the lush jungle world of Haven Tor, a bright and curious hummingbird named Doodle makes a troubling discovery: a mysterious intruder has been stealing the Keystones, the magical gems that allow travel through the gates linking the land's many realms. Alarmed, Doodle rushes to wake her steadfast companion, a coral snake named Noodle, and together the unlikely duo set off to track down the thief and restore order to their home. Their journey carries them through vibrant realms themed around jungle, water, lava, and wind, winding ever upward toward the summit of Haven Tor. Along the way, the pair catch fleeting glimpses of a large flying figure and scattered black feathers, hints of the culprit behind the thefts, as they slither and climb through one treacherous world after another.
