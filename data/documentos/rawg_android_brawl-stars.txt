DESCRIPCIÓN:
Brawl Stars is a fast-paced multiplayer mobile game from Supercell that mixes MOBA and third-person shooter elements. Players choose from a large roster of characters called Brawlers, each with unique weapons and special abilities, and compete in short team-based game modes like Gem Grab, Brawl Ball, Heist and Showdown.
