DESCRIPCIÓN:
Subway Surfers is an endless runner where players dash along train tracks, dodging obstacles and oncoming trains while a grumpy inspector and his dog give chase. Players collect coins to unlock characters, hoverboards and power-ups, and the game regularly rotates its setting to a different real-world city through limited-time updates.
