RESUMEN:
The wicked Grape and the Sour Bunch have taken over the Sparkle Theater, and now it's up to Peach and the theater's guardian Stella to save the play––and the day!

Peach can transform and use fantastic showstopping abilities to face off against the Sour Bunch.

Fend off the Sour Bunch with sensational swordplay as a swordfighter...en garde! In another role, Peach puts on her detective hat to find out whodunnit in a museum mystery. From kung fu master to pastry chef, Peach’s roles give you distinctly powerful ways to save the play!

HISTORIA:
Princess Peach visits the dazzling Sparkle Theater, only for the wicked sorceress Grape and her henchmen, the Sour Bunch, to storm the building and seize control, trapping the audience and performers inside. There Peach meets Stella, the theater's guardian, and the two join forces to take it back. Stepping into the theater's many stage plays, Peach takes on a host of starring roles, from swordfighter and detective to ninja and figure skater, using each new guise to rescue the captured performers and drive Grape's minions from the stage.
