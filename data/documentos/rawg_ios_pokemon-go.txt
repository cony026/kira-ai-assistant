DESCRIPCIÓN:
Pokémon GO is a location-based augmented reality game that uses a player's real-world movement and GPS position to find, catch and train Pokémon at real locations mapped as PokéStops and Gyms. It includes cooperative Raid battles, in-person trading, and regular real-world community events tied to in-game bonuses.
