RESUMEN:
Amnesia: Rebirth is a new descent into darkness from the creators of the iconic Amnesia series. Set in the desolate landscape of the Algerian desert, the game will focus on new character Tasi Trianon as she sets out on a harrowing journey through devastation and despair, personal terror and pain, while exploring the limits of human resilience.

HISTORIA:
Amnesia: Rebirth is a first-person survival horror game set in 1937. Players take on the role of Anastasie "Tasi" Trianon, a French woman whose expedition plane crashes in the Algerian desert, leaving her stranded and unable to recall how she survived. Alone and searching for her missing companions, Tasi presses through sun-scorched caves and ancient ruins, piecing together the terrifying events that unfolded while fighting to hold on to her sanity in the dark. As she descends deeper, she confronts monstrous horrors and buried memories, driven forward by a fierce, desperate resolve to protect what matters most to her.
