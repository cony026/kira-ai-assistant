DESCRIPCIÓN:
Mobile Legends: Bang Bang is a 5v5 MOBA designed for short mobile sessions, where two teams of players each control a hero with unique abilities and battle across lanes to destroy the enemy base. It features a large and regularly updated hero roster, ranked competitive play, and an active global esports scene.
