Over 300+ playable Marvel Super Heroes and Villains await in this multiverse-spanning epic showdown! Collect your favorite characters from teams such as the Avengers, X-Men, Fantastic Four, Spider-Verse, Thunderbolts, Guardians of the Galaxy and more as you compete in world-wide challenges and original monthly adventures!
