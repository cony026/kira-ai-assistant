RESUMEN:
Following the epic storylines of Jurassic Park, The Lost World: Jurassic Park and Jurassic Park III, as well as Jurassic World, LEGO Jurassic World is the first videogame where players will be able to relive and experience all four Jurassic films.

HISTORIA:
This LEGO adventure retells the stories of the first four films in the Jurassic Park saga in a lighthearted, brick-built style. Players relive the fateful visits to islands where cloned dinosaurs have been brought back to life for a grand theme park, only for the prehistoric creatures to slip beyond human control. Guiding familiar faces like Dr. Alan Grant, Ian Malcolm, and Owen Grady, they navigate encounters with dinosaurs ranging from gentle giants to fearsome predators. The famous events are reimagined with humor and playful charm.
