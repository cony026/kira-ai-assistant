RESUMEN:
Moonlighter is an Action RPG with rogue-lite elements that demonstrates two sides of the coin – revealing everyday routines of Will, an adventurous shopkeeper that secretly dreams of becoming a hero.

HISTORIA:
Near the village of Rynoka stand a series of ancient Gates leading to mysterious realms filled with valuable relics and dangerous creatures. Will, a young shopkeeper who dreams of becoming a hero, ventures into these dungeons at night while managing his family’s shop by day. His expeditions gradually draw him toward the secrets hidden beyond the Gates and the long-sealed fifth dungeon.
