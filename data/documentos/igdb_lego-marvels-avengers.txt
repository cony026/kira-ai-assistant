RESUMEN:
Lego Marvel's Avengers is a Lego-themed action-adventure video game developed by Traveller's Tales and published by Warner Bros. Interactive Entertainment. It is the spin-off to Lego Marvel Super Heroes and the second instalment of the Lego Marvel franchise. The game is based on the Marvel Cinematic Universe.

HISTORIA:
This LEGO adventure reimagines the big-screen exploits of Marvel's mightiest heroes in a playful, brick-built style. It centers on Earth's team of Avengers, including Iron Man, Captain America, Thor, the Hulk, Black Widow, and Hawkeye, as they band together against powerful threats to the world. The action draws chiefly from the films The Avengers and Avengers: Age of Ultron, with additional scenes pulled from other adventures across the superhero saga.
