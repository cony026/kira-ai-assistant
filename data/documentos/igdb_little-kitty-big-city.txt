RESUMEN:
You're a curious little kitty with a big personality, on an adventure to find your way back home. Explore the city, make new friends, wear delightful hats, and leave more than a little chaos in your wake. After all, isn't that what cats do best?

HISTORIA:
After tumbling from a high window and finding itself stranded far from home, a curious little black cat must make its way back across a sprawling city to reunite with its owner. As it roams the bustling streets and back alleys, the cat befriends the local animals, takes on their small requests, and gets into plenty of mischief along the way. Bit by bit, it gathers the strength and know-how needed to scale the heights back to its cozy apartment.
