RESUMEN:
Coffee Talk is a coffee brewing and heart-to-heart talking simulator about listening to fantasy-inspired modern peoples’ problems, and helping them by serving up a warm drink or two.

HISTORIA:
Coffee Talk is a cozy visual novel set in an alternate version of modern Seattle, where humans live alongside elves, orcs, mermaids, werewolves, and other fantastical beings. The player takes the role of a late-night barista who runs a small coffee shop, listening to the hopes and worries of the regulars who drift in from the rainy streets. Across a series of evenings, patrons share their personal struggles with love, ambition, prejudice, and belonging, and the drinks the barista brews can gently shape how their conversations unfold. Warm and reflective, the story is less about grand adventure and more about the quiet connections formed over a well-made cup of coffee.
