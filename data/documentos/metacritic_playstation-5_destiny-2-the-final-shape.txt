DESCRIPCIÓN / RESEÑA:
In Destiny 2: The Final Shape, Guardians confront oblivion in the form of their ultimate adversary –The Witness – alongside their iconic Vanguard companions: Ikora Rey, Commander Zavala, and the mysteriously returned Cayde-6. Players embark on a perilous journey of remembrance and discovery into the heart of the Traveler, rally the Vanguard, and end the War of Light and Darkness. The story of this final confrontation is told throughout the course of The Final Shape’s campaign, as well as in the new raid that launches in March 2024.
