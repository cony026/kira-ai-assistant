RESUMEN:
NextWorld2 is a fantasy MMORPG set in the divided world of Kigan, where two rival kingdoms, Selakim and Quila, struggle for dominance. Players can choose from four classes: Warrior, Shaman, Assassin, and Ezra, and explore a vast open world filled with quests, mounts, dungeons, and large-scale community events.

The game emphasizes real-time combat, crafting, exploration, and both PvE and PvP content. Currently available in Hungary, NextWorld2 has thousands of active players and continues to grow with new content and future international expansion plans.

HISTORIA:
The world of NextWorld2 is set in Kigan, the largest continent of a once united realm now divided between two great kingdoms, Selakim and Quila. Long ago, the ancient war between light and shadow shattered the balance of the world, giving birth to endless conflict. The magical force known as the Essence still lingers, empowering both heroes and creatures alike.

Centuries after the fall of the old empires, peace has become fragile. Orc tribes raid borderlands, dark beasts roam ancient ruins, and forgotten powers stir beneath the earth. As tensions rise between Selakim and Quila, whispers spread of an ancient prophecy foretelling the return of the Demon Reaper.

Players must choose their allegiance, fight back against the rising darkness, and uncover the truth behind the forces threatening to consume Kigan once more.
