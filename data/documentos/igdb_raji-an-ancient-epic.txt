RESUMEN:
Raji: An Ancient Epic is an action-adventure game set in ancient India. A young girl named Raji has been chosen by the gods to stand against the demonic invasion of the human realm. Her destiny? To rescue her younger brother and face the demon lord Mahabalasura.

HISTORIA:
Set in an ancient India drawn from Hindu mythology, the story follows Raji, an orphaned circus performer whose only family is her younger brother Golu. On the day of Raksha Bandhan, a demon army descends on their carnival and lays waste to the land, carrying Golu away. Watched over by the goddess Durga and the god Vishnu, Raji is granted a divine trishula and other celestial weapons and sets out across ruined temples, cities, and deserts to rescue her brother and confront the forces behind the invasion.
