RESUMEN:
Touhou Luna Night's is a 2D search action (Metroidvania) video game.
After creating many action games, this is a new project from Team Ladybug.

HISTORIA:
Remilia Scarlet, head vampire of the Scarlet Devil Mansion sends her maid Sakuya Izayoi with no warning to a world much like Gensoukyou, but not quite.

A world full of strange places, filled with strange youkai and her time manipulation sealed.
What could Remilia’s end game be...?
