RESUMEN:
LEGO Horizon Adventures is an action-adventure game developed by Guerrilla Games and Studio Gobo, blending the Horizon series setting with LEGO-style visuals and humour.
Players follow a retelling of Aloy’s story as she travels across a post-apocalyptic world inhabited by machine creatures. Gameplay includes exploration, combat, puzzle-solving, and cooperative play.

HISTORIA:
The Horizon hero becomes a LEGO legend! Found as a baby in a cave and raised by a great machine hunter called Rost, Aloy is curious about her origins and sets off on a journey to discover her destiny. Along the way, she needs to take on a group of sun-worshippers and uncover ancient secrets as her personal quest grows into an epic battle to save the world.
