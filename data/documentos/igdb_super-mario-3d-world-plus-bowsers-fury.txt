RESUMEN:
The Super Mario 3D World + Bowser’s Fury game features the same lively co-op gameplay, creative levels and power-ups as the original game, but with added improvements. In the Super Mario 3D World part of the game, characters move faster and the dash powers up more quickly. Both adventures support the newly added Snapshot Mode, pause the action to get the perfect shot, apply filters, and decorate with stamps!

HISTORIA:
One peaceful evening, Mario, Luigi, Princess Peach, and Toad are enjoying a fireworks show near Peach's Castle when they stumble upon a strange, tilted glass pipe. After Mario and Luigi set it upright, a small green fairy-like Sprixie princess emerges in a panic, warning them that the villainous Bowser has kidnapped six of her fellow Sprixie princesses and sealed them away inside jars. Before the heroes can react, Bowser bursts forth, snatches the last Sprixie, and escapes down the pipe. Determined to rescue her and the others, the four friends dive in after him and find themselves in an unfamiliar land known as the Sprixie Kingdom. Across its many colorful and treacherous worlds, they set out to free the captured princesses and thwart Bowser's latest scheme.
