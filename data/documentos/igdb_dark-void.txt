RESUMEN:
Dark Void is a sci-fi action-adventure game that combines an adrenaline-fueled blend of aerial and ground-pounding combat. Set in a parallel universe called "The Void", players take on the role of Will, a pilot dropped into incredible circumstances within the mysterious Void.

HISTORIA:
Set in the years just before World War II, the story follows William Augustus Grey, a rugged cargo pilot whose routine flight takes a terrifying turn when he passes through the fabled Bermuda Triangle and is suddenly torn from his world. He awakens in a strange parallel dimension known as the Void, a realm caught between worlds. There, Will discovers he is not alone: the Void is home to a menacing alien race called the Watchers, as well as a community of stranded humans known as the Survivors, who have long been locked in a desperate struggle against their otherworldly oppressors. Driven by a single burning desire, to find a way home to Earth, Will reluctantly throws in his lot with the Survivors, taking up their fight against the Watchers as he searches for an escape from the Void.
