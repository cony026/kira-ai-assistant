RESUMEN:
A deep, interactive adventure about love and loss, beautifully depicted and offering an intense cinematic experience. What would you do to save the one you love?

HISTORIA:
Last Day of June is centred around a couple called Carl and June, who suffer a tragedy when a car accident takes June's life and leaves Carl in a wheelchair. Carl then explores past memories of his life with June and attempts to solve puzzles to change the sequence of events that led up to June's death.
