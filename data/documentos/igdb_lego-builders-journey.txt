RESUMEN:
A story about play, connections and adventure.

Step through levels brick by brick with puzzles that ask us to sometimes follow the instructions… and sometimes to be creative and break the rules.

Builder’s Journey is a poetic puzzle that takes place in a LEGO brick world, brought to life with the most accurately rendered LEGO elements yet to feature on screens. Be taken through a breathtaking world filled with brick-by-brick effects, accompanied by a beautiful soundtrack.

Throughout the narrative, there will be ups and downs, challenges and celebrations. Take the time to experiment, and most importantly, to play as figuring out who we are and what we become is the Builder’s Journey.

HISTORIA:
Told entirely without words, this gentle and heartfelt tale follows a small brick-built parent and child as they make their way together through a series of intimate, diorama-like worlds. Set among softly lit landscapes assembled from familiar toy bricks, the journey unfolds as a quiet meditation on family, creativity, and the simple joy of play. As the pair travel onward, the child longs to explore, imagine, and build freely, while the parent is pulled between the demands of a structured, grown-up world and the wish to share in that sense of wonder. Guided only by mood, music, and evocative imagery, the story invites players to interpret its meaning for themselves, piecing together an emotional arc about growing up, letting go, and reconnecting. Each scene becomes both a puzzle to solve and a small moment in a larger relationship, celebrating the timeless spirit of building something beautiful, brick by brick.
