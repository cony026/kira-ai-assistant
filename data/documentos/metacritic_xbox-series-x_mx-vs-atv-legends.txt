MX vs ATV Legends is the latest installment within the MX vs ATV offroad racing universe. Players live the fantasy of becoming a world-famous, professional offroad rider. They´ll bask in the glory of fame and the adulation of both fans and sponsors alike while playing the career and online modes. Race in 2 player split-screen and 16-player online mode with squad-based gameplay. Experience the brand new Trails mode for high intensity outdoor racing, - each turn can present new surprises, challenging your drivestyle in new ways.
