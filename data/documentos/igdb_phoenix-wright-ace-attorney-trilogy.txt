Join rookie lawyer Phoenix Wright through the early years of his career in his quest to find the truth behind all of the classic cases! HISTORIA:
Phoenix Wright is a rookie defense attorney with an unshakable belief in his clients and a knack for turning hopeless cases around. Across three interconnected chapters of courtroom drama, he takes on a string of seemingly unwinnable trials, defending clients that everyone else has already written off as guilty. Working from crime scenes to the courtroom, Phoenix gathers evidence, interviews witnesses, and picks apart shaky testimony to expose the contradictions hiding within it. Standing in his way are sharp, theatrical prosecutors determined to secure a conviction at any cost, while allies such as the spirited assistant Maya Fey and Phoenix's own rival, the brilliant Miles Edgeworth, weave in and out of his life. Each case begins as a simple defense but spirals into tangled webs of deception, hidden motives, and long-buried secrets. To win, Phoenix must uncover the truth one objection at a time, no matter how impossible the odds appear.
