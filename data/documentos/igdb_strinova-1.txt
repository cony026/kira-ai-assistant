RESUMEN:
Strinova is an anime-style third-person tactical competitive shooter developed by Day 1 Studio. Switch freely between three-dimensional and two-dimensional in this one-of-a-kind shooter experience with a unique tactical shooting system.

HISTORIA:
Due to the disasters of the old world, humanity has chosen to migrate to a multidimensional space called Strinova, where Superstrings, adhering to different ideologies, fight for their beliefs. You will become a beacon, a pioneer to the future of humanity, and find your direction in Strinova...
