RESUMEN:
Supermarket Simulator is a first-person business management game where players run and expand their own grocery store. Gameplay involves stocking shelves, managing a cash register, processing online orders, and designing the store layout. Players set prices based on fluctuating market conditions. A loan mechanic tied to a mafia storyline adds pressure through mandatory nightly repayments, creating consequences for poor financial performance.
