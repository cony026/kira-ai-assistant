DESCRIPCIÓN / RESEÑA:
Save kittens. It&#39;s that simple. Armed with the latest in kitten-saving technology, it&#39;s your duty to stop them from plummeting to their inevitable doom. Watch as countless kittens fall from the rooftops, and you their only hope. Use your cardboard box to catch them before they hit the pavement, but lose nine cats and it&#39;s over.

Compare your kitten saving skills against the world with online leaderboards for four different difficulties, ranging from Easy to Insane. 

How to Play:
-Drag the cardboard box under kittens to catch them.
-Every fifty kittens caught gives you an extra life.
-Increase your score multiplier by catching ten kittens in a row.
