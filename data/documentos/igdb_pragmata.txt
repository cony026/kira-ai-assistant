RESUMEN:
Pragmata is a sci-fi action-adventure game set in the near future. The story follows Hugh and his android companion Diana as they navigate a Lunar research station. Gameplay incorporates action elements alongside mechanics involving interaction with systems within the environment.

HISTORIA:
In the near future, the Delphi Corporation establishes a lunar mining outpost called the Cradle to harvest lunafilament, a material essential for their revolutionary 3D printing technology. When communications with the facility go silent, systems engineer Hugh Williams is sent to investigate. Upon arrival, he finds the station abandoned and its AI administrator, IDUS, has turned hostile. Rescued by a child-like android named Diana, Hugh must fight his way through the Cradle's robotic workforce to uncover what happened and find a way to send a distress signal back to Earth.
