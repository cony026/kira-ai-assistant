RESUMEN:
Rise as the Dark Knight and experience the essential Batman story in a bold, action-packed adventure with hard-hitting combat, an open-world Gotham City, and the signature LEGO charm that fans know and love.

HISTORIA:
After witnessing his parents' murder as a child, Bruce Wayne trains with the League of Shadows before returning to Gotham City to fight crime as Batman. Alongside allies including Jim Gordon, Catwoman, Robin, Batgirl, and Nightwing, he confronts a growing roster of villains threatening the city, from the Falcone crime family to the Joker and beyond.
