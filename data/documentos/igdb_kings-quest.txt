RESUMEN:
King’s Quest is a collection of five different chapter releases, each focusing on an untold tale in Graham’s past. In each chapter, an aging King Graham reflects on his life of adventure with his granddaughter, Gwendolyn, while players are transported back to influence first-hand the decisions and events that shaped a kingdom. Embark on an extraordinary journey across the world of Daventry.

HISTORIA:
King’s Quest: The Complete Collection invites players on a whimsical and heartfelt journey through the legendary land of Daventry. Told through the reflective lens of an aging King Graham, the game unfolds across five unique chapters—each chronicling an untold adventure from his youth. As Graham recounts these tales to his curious granddaughter Gwendolyn, players relive daring quests, clever puzzles, and moments of bravery, shaping both a kingdom’s legacy and a family’s bond.
Blending classic point-and-click charm with modern storytelling, each episode offers a distinct glimpse into Graham’s evolving character, his triumphs and trials, and the values that guided him—courage, wit, and compassion. This reimagining of the iconic franchise celebrates the spirit of adventure and the power of storytelling across generations.
