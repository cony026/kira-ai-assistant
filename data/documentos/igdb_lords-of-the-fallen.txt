RESUMEN:
Lords of the Fallen is a fantasy action role-playing game set in a world where humanity has overthrown its god. The story follows Harkyn, a convicted criminal chosen to confront the returning demonic forces known as the Rhogar and their leaders, the Lords.

The game focuses on melee combat, with weapons, armor, and character attributes affecting movement, defense, and attack style. Players explore interconnected areas, fight enemies and bosses, collect equipment, and use magic alongside physical attacks. It was developed by CI Games in collaboration with Deck13.

HISTORIA:
When the tyrannical god Adyr invades the human realm in search of vengeance for his imprisonment, humanity calls upon an unlikely defender: a convicted sinner named Harkyn.
