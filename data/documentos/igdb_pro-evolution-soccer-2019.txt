RESUMEN:
Pro Evolution Soccer 2019 is a football simulation game developed and published by Konami in 2018. It emphasizes realistic gameplay with improved ball physics, player fatigue, and enhanced animations. The game expands its roster of licensed leagues and clubs, while maintaining modes such as Master League and Become a Legend.
