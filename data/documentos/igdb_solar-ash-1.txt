HISTORIA:
Rei is a Voidrunner racing to save her home planet from destruction as it is drawn toward the Ultravoid, an immense, all-consuming black hole. Venturing into this surreal, neon-hued cosmic realm, she skates and soars across dreamlike drifting landscapes populated by colossal creatures called Remnants. To hold back her world's doom, Rei must scale and slay these titanic beasts while unraveling the mysteries of the Ultravoid and the desperate hope that led her there.
