RESUMEN:
Shoot your way through 23 rotation maps to earn rewards for your efforts! Master the highly skill-based slidehop movement system unique to Krunker. If dropping nukes and quick-scoping people in pubs isn't your thing, Krunker also offers thousands of custom games to choose from which includes Infected, parkour, free for all, capture the flag and much much more. With Krunker's robust modding and mapping tools there are no limits to what you can create and experience.
