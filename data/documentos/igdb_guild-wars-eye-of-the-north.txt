RESUMEN:
Guild Wars: Eye of the North is the first expansion for Guild Wars with content geared exclusively for level 20 characters.

Return to the battle-scarred continent of Tyria, the home of the original Guild Wars game. There, old friends await, ready to assist you in settling past scores. But an even greater threat looms... The land has erupted, spewing forth a race of beings with only one thought—destroy everything. Win or lose, your actions will set the stage for Guild Wars 2.
