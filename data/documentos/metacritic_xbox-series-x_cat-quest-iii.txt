DESCRIPCIÓN / RESEÑA:
Get whiskered away on a hearty catventure in Cat Quest: Pirates of the Purribean, the latest instalment in the award-winning Cat Quest series!

Play as a swashbuckling purrivateer in this 2.5D open-world action RPG set in a fantastical pirate-themed world - the Purribean - an archipelago swarming with Pi-rats searching for the Northern Star - a long-lost mythical treasure. 

Alongside your trusty spirit companion, set sail through the Purribean in your very own ship! But beware, the seas are dangerous, and a meow-tiny is nigh as the hordes of Pi-rats under the order of the Pirate King hunt you down...
