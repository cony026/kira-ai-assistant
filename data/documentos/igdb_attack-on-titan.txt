RESUMEN:
Attack on Titan closely follows the riveting story line of Attack on Titan’s first season and focuses on the exploits of various key characters, putting the player in a position to relive the anime’s most shocking, courageous, and exhilarating moments. It revolves around the story of three young people who survive the destruction of their walled city district by enormous, man-eating Titans and eventually go on to join the Scout Regiment in order to protect humanity from this overpowering foe.

HISTORIA:
For a century, the last remnants of humanity have sheltered behind towering walls that keep out the Titans, enormous humanoid creatures that devour people without reason. That fragile peace is shattered when a monstrous Colossal Titan breaks through the outer wall, letting the giants pour in and overrun the city. Among the survivors is the young Eren Yeager, who loses his home and family in the assault and swears to wipe out every last Titan. Enlisting alongside his friends in the military's elite Scout Regiment, he ventures beyond the walls to fight the creatures and uncover the truth behind them.
