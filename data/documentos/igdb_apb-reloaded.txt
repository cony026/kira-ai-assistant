RESUMEN:
The world’s first and premier Action MMO Third Person Shooter allows you to choose between two sides of the law. Play as Enforcer or Criminal, customize your gear for the task at hand and hit the streets and play how you want in a city filled with more action this side of a Hollywood blockbuster.

It was originally released as APB: All Points Bulletin in 2010 before relaunching as a free-to-play game.

It was released on Xbox One in 2016 and PS4 in 2017. In 2024 both console versions were delisted as Little Orbit and PLAION decided to sunset APB Reloaded on console platforms. Console players can migrate their characters to PC as the servers for the console versions go offline.
