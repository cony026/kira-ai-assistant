DESCRIPCIÓN:
Monopoly GO! is a mobile reimagining of the classic Monopoly board game, built around single-player board runs instead of a traditional multiplayer match. Players roll dice to move around the board, build sets of properties, and take part in limited-time events and mini-games with friends, such as raiding shields or trading stickers to complete albums.
