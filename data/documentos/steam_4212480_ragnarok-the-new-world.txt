RESEÑA / DESCRIPCIÓN:
Ragnarok: The New World is Gravity's officially licensed cross-platform MMORPG, reimagining RO's cute, classic Midgard with high-quality visuals and audio on PC and mobile. Choose 8 Classes, including the new Druid. Explore, tame MVPs as Mounts, trade freely, and customize 100+ looks with friends!

DESCRIPCIÓN DETALLADA:
The first open-world RO! This time, the world is unimaginably vast! Every scene is a grandiose view, and every step an adventure! Let’s journey across time, go beyond the classics, and meet in the New World. This time, Dare to Play Different! Please stay tuned for an official announcement regarding the Steam version’s release time.

[Seamless Across Three Devices, Take the World with You]
- Switch seamlessly between your phone, tablet, and PC anytime, with your progress perfectly synced—sneak in a session at work, kick back and play at home, or tackle new content late into the night. One world has it all.
- Enjoy PC-grade visuals with performance fully unleashed and support for an ultra-smooth 120 FPS experience! No delays in open-world PVP, no stutters during dungeon combos, skills always at your command, and the entire world just a tap away!

[Win Easy with Grindless Gaming]
-Free stat resets forever! Free resource retrievals! Say goodbye to grinding and spending. Enjoy the world stress-free!
-Refine Safely without Breaking: With no level loss on failure, your gear stays intact! Reach +15 with confidence. No more hitting that Refine button in fear!
-Earn Resources While Offline: While you sleep, your character continues the hunt! While you work, resources quietly pile up. Idling automatically accumulates resources, making you stronger even when you're resting!

[Change Jobs Anytime]
-Be a tank and get chased by a boss today, heal your teammates tomorrow—play however you like! Your adventurer will never need a do-over!
-Thief, Swordman, Mage... Master every Class with just one character. Skill resets are always free, so you'll never pick a wrong Class!
