RESUMEN:
Team Sonic Racing combines the best elements of arcade and fast-paced competitive style racing as you face-off with friends in intense multiplayer racing.

HISTORIA:
A mysterious alien tanuki named Dodon Pa sends invitations to Sonic the Hedgehog and a host of his friends and rivals, summoning them to compete in a grand series of team-based races. As a prize, he offers each racer a custom-built car packed with advanced technology, tailored to their individual style. Though intrigued by the challenge, Sonic and the others cannot shake their suspicions about the eccentric stranger and his true motives, especially with the ever-scheming Doctor Eggman lurking nearby. Divided into teams, characters like Sonic, Tails, Knuckles, Amy, Shadow, and Blaze must set aside their differences and learn to cooperate, sharing boosts and power-ups to cross the finish line together rather than racing solely for themselves. The competition carries them across a variety of vibrant, high-speed courses, and as the races grow more demanding, the group begins to wonder what Dodon Pa is really after.
