DESCRIPCIÓN / RESEÑA:
Ghostrunner 2 is the highly anticipated hardcore FPP slasher set one year after the events of Ghostrunner. 

Blood will run in this post-apocalyptic cyberpunk future that takes place after the fall of the Keymaster, a tyrant who ruled over Dharma Tower, the last refuge of humanity. The player has the power to shape the future of humanity and take on the violent cyber ninja AI cult that has taken shape outside Dharma Tower, making for a truly epic and unforgettable experience.
