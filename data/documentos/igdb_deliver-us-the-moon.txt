RESUMEN:
Deliver Us the Moon is a sci-fi adventure game developed by KeokeN Interactive and released in 2019. Set in a near future where Earth's resources are nearly depleted, the game follows a lone astronaut on a mission to the Moon to restore energy to the planet. Players explore abandoned lunar bases and use tools to solve environmental puzzles while uncovering the fate of the people who once lived there. The game combines narrative-driven exploration with light puzzle-solving elements and offers a mix of first-person and third-person perspectives.

HISTORIA:
In the near future, Earth's natural resources have run dry, and humanity's survival now depends on a vital energy supply mined on the Moon. When that lunar supply suddenly goes silent, plunging the world toward extinction, a lone astronaut named Rolf is launched on a desperate solo mission to uncover what went wrong. Exploring the eerily abandoned lunar facilities, he pieces together the fate of the colonists who vanished and races to restore the connection that keeps his dying planet alive. What he discovers among the silent stations carries the weight of humanity's last hope.
