RESUMEN:
Distance is a survival racing game where you pilot a versatile car through a mysterious, ruined world, using boost, jump, rotate, and flight abilities to navigate treacherous tracks and uncover the story of its past. It blends arcade-style racing with exploration, offering solo adventure, multiplayer modes, a built-in level editor with Steam Workshop support, and an original soundtrack.
