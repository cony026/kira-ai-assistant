RESUMEN:
Pinstripe is an emotionally charged adventure about Hell created entirely by a one-man team over the course of five years. Play as Teddy, an estranged ex-minister forced to venture through the frozen afterlife in search of Bo, his three year old daughter, and her perverse kidnapper. Discover the dark secrets of Teddy's past, and confront his sleazy, demonic nemesis, Mr. Pinstripe.

HISTORIA:
Teddy, a disgraced former minister, is riding a train with his three-year-old daughter Bo when a sinister shadowy figure named Pinstripe abducts her. When the train crashes into a strange, snow-covered town, Teddy sets off through its haunting, hellish reaches to find her. Armed with Bo's slingshot and accompanied by his dog George, he pushes through a world of drunken, sorrowful residents in pursuit of Pinstripe, confronting his own past as he searches for his daughter.
