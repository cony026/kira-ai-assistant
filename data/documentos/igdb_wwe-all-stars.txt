RESUMEN:
In WWE All Stars, icons past and present collide to produce piledriving arcade action that up to four players can be a part of. Choose to grapple with greats of yesteryear like Hulk Hogan, “Macho Man” Randy Savage and Ultimate Warrior, or bring modern moves to the squared circle with stars of today such as Big Show, Triple H and John Cena.
