RESUMEN:
Alan Wake II: Night Springs is a DLC expansion for Alan Wake II. It adds new story content that delves into mysterious broadcasts and eerie environments connected to the Night Springs phenomenon. The expansion continues the base game's focus on psychological horror and narrative-driven exploration.
