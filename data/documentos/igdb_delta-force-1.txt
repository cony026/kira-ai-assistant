RESUMEN:
Delta Force is a free-to-play tactical first-person shooter developed by Team Jade and published by TiMi Studio Group. A reboot of the classic series originally created by NovaLogic, the game features large-scale multiplayer modes including a Warfare mode for massive PvP battles and Hazard Operations, an extraction-style mode. Players choose from a roster of operators with unique abilities fitting into Assault, Support, Engineer, or Recon archetypes. The game also includes a single-player and cooperative campaign inspired by the film Black Hawk Down, recreating Operation Gothic Serpent.

HISTORIA:
Set in 2035, the game takes place in Ahsarah, a fictional North African nation where an energy crisis led the government to partner with Haavk, a powerful technology corporation. When Haavk begins expanding its control beyond the original agreement, the nationalist Ahsarah Guard faction revolts. The Global Threat Initiative (GTI), a paramilitary peacekeeping force, deploys to restore order, creating a three-way conflict between all parties. Players take the role of GTI operatives working to uncover Haavk's true ambitions, which include a mind-control program called Relink and a satellite network capable of disabling global communications.
