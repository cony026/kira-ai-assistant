DESCRIPCIÓN:
PUBG Mobile is the official mobile adaptation of PlayerUnknown's Battlegrounds, dropping up to 100 players onto a large map to scavenge weapons and gear while the safe zone gradually shrinks, until only one player or squad remains. It features multiple maps, vehicles, and both classic and arcade-style game modes.
