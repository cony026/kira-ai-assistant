RESUMEN:
In Mini Royale, up to 50 toy soldiers fight for victory. The game is played in a children's bedroom, from a rather unusual perspective, full of exciting things to discover, and lots of weapons and equipment that will help you set yourself apart from other players.

HISTORIA:
It was a dark and stormy night. The wind howled and the rain lashed against the windows. Inside, the 50 toy soldiers prepared for battle. They knew that only one could emerge victorious from the Mini Royale.

The battle was fierce. Bullets flew and explosions rang out. The ground was soon littered with fallen soldiers. As the numbers dwindled, the fighting became more desparate. The survivors knew that there could only be one winner.

Finally, there were only two soldiers left standing. They faced each other across the bedroom field, both exhausted but still determinded to win.

All you could hear is the sound of a rifle - and is was quiet. The battle was over.
