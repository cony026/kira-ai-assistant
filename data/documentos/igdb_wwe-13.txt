RESUMEN:
WWE ’13 transforms WWE gameplay through the introduction of WWE Live, completely changing the way players experience the videogame’s audio and presentation elements. Predator Technology returns to further implement critical gameplay improvements, while fan favourites in WWE Universe Mode and the franchise’s renowned Creation Suite offer the utmost in player freedom.

Furthermore, WWE ’13 focuses on the Attitude Era of the WWF, replacing its Road to WrestleMania Mode with an Attitude Era Mode where the player plays through the Attitude Era. The game gives such characters as Stone Cold Steve Austin and elements as the Monday Night Wars a more important role in the game than in any previous WWE game.
