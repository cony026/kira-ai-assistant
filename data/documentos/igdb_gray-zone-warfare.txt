RESUMEN:
Enter a high-stakes PvE-first open-world tactical shooter set on a quarantined Southeast Asian island torn apart by faction warfare. Deploy as an elite operator, fight solo or with your squad to carry out missions, earn reputation, and explore the truth behind the Event igniting the conflict.

HISTORIA:
Following a mysterious event on the fictional southeast Asian island of Lamang, the entire region has been placed under international quarantine. With civilians evacutated by the United Nations, three private miltiary companies, Mithras Security Systems, Crimson Shield International and Lamang Recovery Initiative, are deployed to explore the zone, uncover its secrets and bring back anything of value.
