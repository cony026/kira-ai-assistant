GÉNEROS: Action, Shooter, Massively Multiplayer
TAGS: Free to Play, Battle Royale, Multiplayer, PvP, First-Person Shooter

DESCRIPCIÓN:
Call of Duty: Mobile brings series-standard multiplayer maps, weapons and characters from across the Call of Duty franchise to mobile devices, alongside a dedicated 100-player Battle Royale mode. It supports controller input and cross-progression features, and runs regular seasonal content updates similar to the console entries.
