The game that all One Piece fans have been waiting for. One Piece Burning Blood is a dynamic and colourful new fighting game featuring the worlds most famous pirate: Monkey D. Luffy.

HISTORIA:
The Paramount War mode acts as One Piece: Burning Blood’s story mode. Playing as either Luffy, Whitebeard, Akainu or Ace, you go through a series of missions from their own perspectives with some side missions unlocked along the way.
