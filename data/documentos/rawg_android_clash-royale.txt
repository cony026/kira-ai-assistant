DESCRIPCIÓN:
Clash Royale is a real-time strategy game that blends tower defense, collectible card mechanics and light MOBA elements. Players build a deck of cards representing troops, spells and buildings from the Clash universe, then deploy them in short head-to-head matches to destroy the opponent's towers while defending their own.
