RESUMEN:
Rayman Legends is a side-scrolling platform game supporting up to four simultaneous players. Players control Rayman, Globox, Teensies, or the new character Barbara as they run, jump, and attack through over one hundred twenty levels. An assist character named Murfy can interact with the environment by cutting ropes, activating mechanisms, and grabbing enemies. The game features rhythm-based levels set to music, daily and weekly online challenge stages with leaderboards, remastered levels from Rayman Origins, and a local multiplayer football minigame called Kung Foot.

HISTORIA:
A century after the events of Rayman Origins, Rayman, Globox, and the Teensies have been asleep. During their slumber, the Bubble Dreamer's nightmares have grown more powerful with the help of the Magician, who survived his previous defeat and split himself into five Dark Teensies. The Dark Teensies have captured Barbara and her fellow warrior princesses. When Murfy discovers what has happened, he wakes Rayman and his companions, and together they set out across multiple worlds to end the chaos and rescue the princesses.
