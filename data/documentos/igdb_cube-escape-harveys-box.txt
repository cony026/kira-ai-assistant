Cube Escape: Harvey’s Box is the fourth episode of the Cube Escape series and part of the Rusty Lake story.

Harvey the parrot is moving to Rusty Lake inside of a cardboard box. Help Harvey find a way out by discovering the purpose of the odd items scattered around the box: a fruit, a comb and a box of cigars...
