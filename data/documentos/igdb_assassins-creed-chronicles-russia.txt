HISTORIA:
Set in early twentieth-century Russia in the turbulent aftermath of the 1917 October Revolution, the story follows Nikolai Orelov, a weary member of the Assassin Brotherhood. In 1918, Nikolai dreams of retiring and leaving the country with his family, but first agrees to carry out one final mission: to recover a powerful artifact known as the Precursor box, believed to be in the hands of the Romanov family. He travels to Yekaterinburg and infiltrates the house where the former Tsar Nicholas II and his family are being held, only to witness their execution at the hands of Templar agents hidden within the Red Army, who are also hunting the box. In the aftermath, Nikolai discovers that the Tsar's youngest daughter, Anastasia, has survived and now carries the mysterious artifact, drawing him into a desperate flight through a nation torn apart by revolution.
