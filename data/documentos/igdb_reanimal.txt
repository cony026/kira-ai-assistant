RESUMEN:
The creators of Little Nightmares I & II have returned to take you on a darker, more terrifying journey than ever before. In this horror adventure game, a brother & sister go through hell to rescue their missing friends and escape the island that they used to call home.

HISTORIA:
A young brother and sister return to the island where they grew up, only to find it flooded, decayed, and scarred by war. Now overrun with grotesque, monstrous creatures, the ruined home holds their three missing friends somewhere within its horrors. Traveling by boat and on foot through drowned villages, a lonely lighthouse, and a dilapidated orphanage, the siblings must hide from and outwit the nightmarish things that hunt them as they search for their friends and confront the dark secrets of their shared past.
