Wiki------
Windjammers is a sports game played from a top-down perspective. Similar to ice hockey, players are tasked to shoot a frisbee at the goal zone of the opponent attempting to score.[1] In the game, players are able to select different stages and characters. Some characters from the original game, including Wessel, returns for the sequel.[2] Each character has their own attributes. For instance, some characters have more steady control than others at the cost of speed.[3] There are EX Moves, which are special powers can be activated for gameplay advantages.[4] Both single-player and local multiplayer modes are featured.[1]
