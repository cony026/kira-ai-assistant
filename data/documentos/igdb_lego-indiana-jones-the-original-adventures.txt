RESUMEN:
Build, Battle and Brawl your way out of trouble! Play through all three classic Indiana Jones movies and relive your favorite Indy adventures in the tongue-in-cheek worlds of LEGO. Explore and Discover - Battle enemies, solve puzzles, and seek out the world's greatest treasures.

HISTORIA:
This LEGO adventure retells the exploits of the adventuring archaeologist Indiana Jones as depicted in his first three film outings. Players guide him and his allies through globe-spanning quests to recover legendary artifacts while staying one step ahead of rival treasure hunters. The familiar events are reimagined in a lighthearted, family-friendly style built from LEGO bricks.
