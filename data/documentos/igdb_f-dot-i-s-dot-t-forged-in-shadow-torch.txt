RESUMEN:
It is a metroidvania game that the player will play as a rabbit who can manipulate the big mech-arm on his back, exploring in a vivid diesel punk world and fighting against the evil robot army. There will be a vast world map for the player to explore to meet with friend NPCs and encounter enemies, discover items and weapons and seek for the answers for the secrets hidden behind.

HISTORIA:
In the dieselpunk metropolis of Torch City, once home to peaceful animal folk, life has fallen under the iron rule of the invading Machine Legion after a devastating war. Rayton, an anthropomorphic rabbit and former combat pilot who fought in that lost conflict, has spent years lying low and trying to forget his past. When events pull him back into the fight, he takes up a massive mechanical fist forged from his old battle armor and ventures into the depths of the occupied city to battle the Legion and uncover the sinister conspiracy lurking beneath it.
