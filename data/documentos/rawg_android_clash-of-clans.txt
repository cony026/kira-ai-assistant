DESCRIPCIÓN:
Clash of Clans is a mobile strategy game where players build and upgrade a village, train troops, and join Clans to attack and defend against other players. Progression revolves around resource management, base design and raiding opponents' villages, alongside recurring Clan Wars and seasonal events.
