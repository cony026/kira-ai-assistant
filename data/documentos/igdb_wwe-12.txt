RESUMEN:
WWE ’12 reinvents the series with smoother animations, deeper match flow, and a new Breaking Point submission system that changes how victories are earned. It also expands customization by letting you design your wrestler’s entrance and the arena they step into, then take your created stars through a long-form Road to Wrestlemania.
The game includes an expanded roster featuring current WWE favorites alongside player-created Superstars with custom entrances, finishing moves, and storylines. Its main campaign spans 18 months of WWE programming and culminates in two WrestleMania championships.
