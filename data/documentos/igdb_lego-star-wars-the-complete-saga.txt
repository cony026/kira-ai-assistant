RESUMEN:
Lego Star Wars: The Complete Saga is a Lego-themed action-adventure game with platforming and puzzle-solving, played from a third-person perspective in a world built from Lego pieces. It combines and updates the content of two earlier games, Lego Star Wars: The Video Game and Lego Star Wars II: The Original Trilogy, covering the six films of the Skywalker Saga across dozens of story levels. Players take on many different characters, each with distinct abilities such as lightsabers and the Force for Jedi and Sith, or blasters and grappling hooks for others, and use those abilities to progress and reach hidden areas. It supports drop-in co-op play, a Cantina hub world for free roaming and character customization, a Free Play mode for replaying levels, and a large amount of collectibles to gather.

HISTORIA:
The game retells the story of the six-film Skywalker Saga through comedic, dialogue-free cutscenes rendered in Lego form. Its levels span the full arc of the saga, beginning with the Trade Federation's blockade and negotiations above the planet Naboo and continuing through to the Rebel assault on the second Death Star near the forest moon of Endor. Along the way, players step into the roles of the saga's many heroes and villains as the conflict between the galaxy's factions unfolds.
